# Privacy Policy — Flamingo (Swiss Army Knife)

**Effective date:** September 10, 2026

## The short version

Flamingo is a **100% offline** utility toolkit. It collects, transmits, and
shares **no personal data**. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising of any kind.

## Permissions and how they're used

Every permission Flamingo requests is used **only on your device, only while
the related tool is open**, and nothing it reads ever leaves the phone:

- **Camera** — QR Scanner (reads barcodes locally) and Magnifier.
  No photos or video are stored or transmitted.
- **Microphone** — Sound Meter (live dB readout), Guitar Tuner, and Voice
  Recorder. Audio is analyzed or saved **locally only**; recordings stay in
  the app's private storage until you delete them.
- **Location (Android 11 and older only)** — required by old Android versions
  to read the compass/magnetometer reliably. Flamingo never reads, stores, or
  shares your location; the permission is not requested at all on Android 12+.
- **Notifications & exact alarms** — Timer, Alarm Clock, and Pomodoro alerts
  fire locally on-device.
- **Flashlight / vibrate** — Flashlight tool and haptic feedback.

## Data storage

Notes, recordings, and settings live in the app's private, on-device storage.
Uninstalling the app deletes them. Nothing is synced, backed up to our
servers (we have none), or shared with third parties.

## Third parties

None. No SDKs, no trackers, no ads, no network calls.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change lands in this repository's public git
history first.
